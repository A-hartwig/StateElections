About This List

Every two years all 435 U.S. House seats are contested, and in 2026 the 33 Senate seats in Class 2 are up for re-election. Before the November general election, each major party holds a primary so that registered voters can choose their nominee.

This site covers the 47 states that hold traditional partisan primaries. Three states are excluded because they use non-partisan ballot systems where all candidates—regardless of party—appear on a single primary ballot:

  • California – top-two primary
  • Washington – top-two primary
  • Alaska – ranked-choice, non-partisan primary

⚠️ This page reflects candidate information as of March 2026. Filing deadlines are still open in most states. Candidates marked TBD – Filing Period Open have not yet officially announced.
